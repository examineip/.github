# Code of Conduct

These projects are small and maintained by one person. The rules are simple.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good faith, including when you disagree.
- Keep discussion about the code, the data and the behaviour — not about people.
- Back technical claims with something checkable: a failing input, a spec reference, a test.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, including promotional links unrelated to the issue.

## Enforcement

Comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules may be edited, hidden or removed, and
repeated or serious cases can lead to a block from the account's repositories.

To report a problem privately, email **contact@examineip.com**. Reports are kept confidential.

This applies in every repository under [github.com/examineip](https://github.com/examineip) and in
any project space where you are representing one of them.
